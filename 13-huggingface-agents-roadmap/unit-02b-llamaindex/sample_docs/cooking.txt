Cooking notes — pasta

For the best dried pasta, salt the water heavily (~1 tbsp per litre). The water
should taste like seawater. Don't add oil — it stops the sauce from sticking
to the noodles later.

Cook ~1 minute less than the package says. Finish in the sauce with a ladle
of pasta water — the starch in the water emulsifies the sauce and makes it
cling. This is the single most important pasta tip.

For carbonara: no cream, ever. Eggs + pecorino + guanciale + black pepper.
The heat from the pasta cooks the egg. Toss vigorously off the heat.
