Travel notes — Lisbon

Three days is enough. Stay in Alfama (the old quarter) for the views.
Walk everywhere — it's a small city built on hills and the trams are
overpriced and slow.

Pastel de Belem is the original pastel de nata, but the queue is huge.
Manteigaria in Chiado is just as good and there's no queue.

Best public transport hack: the day pass (Carris) is cheap and includes
the funiculars, which are the only fast way uphill.
